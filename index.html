About the Community

BeCastedFilms is a non-profit community for teen filmmakers based in Olongapo City, Philippines (Zambales). We support young creators who want to learn and collaborate on independent film projects. This space is for community, practice, and collaboration.

Please note: Participants in BeCastedFilms projects are not paid for shooting. By applying you acknowledge that you understand and accept that participation is voluntary and unpaid.
